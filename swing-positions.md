| Position | Phase                         | Description                                                      |
|----------|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| P1       | Address (Setup)               | The starting position before the swing begins.                   |
| P2       | Takeaway                      | The start of the swing when the club moves away and is parallel to the ground. |
| P3       | Mid Backswing                 | The halfway point of the backswing, when the lead arm is parallel to the ground. |
| P4       | Top of Backswing              | The highest point of the backswing, where the club is often parallel to the ground. |
| P5       | Early Downswing (Transition)  | The transition from the backswing to the downswing.              |
| P6       | Pre-Impact                    | The point just before impact, when the club shaft is parallel to the ground. |
| P7       | Impact                        | The moment the club strikes the ball.                            |
| P8       | Release                       | The post-impact position where the arms extend through the ball. |
| P9       | Follow-Through                | The continuation of the swing after the ball is hit.             |
| P10      | Finish                        | The final, balanced position at the end of the swing.            |
